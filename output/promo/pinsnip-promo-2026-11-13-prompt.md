## 提示词

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create one polished Chinese marketing poster for PinSnip, a native macOS screenshot app. Landscape 3:2, high resolution. This is a designed promotional image, not an app screenshot.
Reference image is the OFFICIAL PinSnip app icon: reproduce its existing design accurately as the brand mark near the top left, do not invent a new logo. Retain the navy rounded square, white photo frame, turquoise mountains, yellow sun, and coral pin badge.
Art direction: refined modern software advertising with warm ivory background, deep navy typography, restrained cobalt blue and turquoise accents taken from the icon, tiny coral details. Crisp editorial typography, very legible simplified Chinese, generous margins, deliberate alignment, restrained soft shadows. No cyberpunk, no busy gradient background, no stock people, no devices or desk props.
Layout: top 35% is a strong typographic header with the official app icon beside the wordmark "PinSnip", followed by a prominent Chinese headline "截图、长图、动图、贴图" and a smaller line "Mac 上的截图工具，一次备齐". Hierarchy should be confident, understated and clean.
Middle 50% is four distinct delightful feature illustrations aligned on a single baseline, organically composed as elegant overlapping paper-like screenshot panels, NOT a generic dashboard grid, no card containers enclosing each feature. Make the FOUR labels clearly mapped to their illustrations:
1 "截图标注": a landscape screenshot selection with four corner crop marks, a coral annotation arrow, a small number marker. Inside is tasteful abstract mountain scenery in turquoise and navy.
2 "滚动长截图": a tall narrow continuous webpage-like strip with several stacked abstract image/text sections and a subtle scroll direction cue; entirely graphical, no gibberish words.
3 "录制动图": three slightly offset frames with consistent abstract scenery, a small red recording dot and a "GIF" badge. Suggest a recorded sequence without a video editing interface.
4 "桌面贴图": two overlapping small floating image panels with a coral pin symbol, demonstrating reference images kept visible.
Keep the illustration style consistent with the official icon: clean geometric illustration, subtly dimensional flat surfaces, rounded corners, no overdone glass. Labels below each illustration in dark navy medium-weight Chinese sans serif. Comfortable spacing, clearly readable on mobile.
Bottom slim footer: "截图暂存 · 图片美化 · 本机处理" at left; "适用于 macOS 14 及以上" at right. Small but legible, no fake button, no QR, no URLs. Include only the exact text specified. Ensure spelling PinSnip exactly. These are conceptual feature illustrations; do not imply additional unsupported features such as OCR, AI, video editing, cloud sync. No pricing claims. Deliver a complete finished advertisement, no surrounding frame or watermark.
